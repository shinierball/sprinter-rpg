# Changelog

## 8.4.0

Worlds from 8.0.0 or later upgrade when the GM loads them. This release converts one thing: Playing to the Cameras chat cards named their result by one of four row numbers, and now name it by one of the table's six results (an old "fail" row is read from the card's own roll as a Near Miss, Standard Fail or Major Fail). An old card that had already paid out is closed, so it never pays again under the new table. An old card's choice that was never answered says to make it by hand.

Worlds that imported the books: re-run the import on this release and accept its sync offer, so the imported documents and the crawlers' copies of them pick up this release's fixes. Earth Classes now require an Earth Race (another race applying one asks a Rule Break). The Endurance Rank 10 and Swimming Rank 5 and 15 upgrades no longer put Fatigued on the target, since they describe the user's own Fatigue, and a Rank 15 upgrade that keeps its holder from dying no longer lists Dying as a debuff it inflicts. The same sync moves a skill's Limitations out of its notes into the new Limitations field, adds this release's forced movement, reload, scatter and ammunition rows to the book's skills, and makes the Tutorial Floor neighbourhood grinding tables roll tables the Grind window can pick.

- **Leaner sheet tables.** The Spells, Skills and Inventory tables, and the page-1 Spells & Magic table, show fewer columns by default, and a Columns menu on each picks what shows, for this sheet or as your default for every sheet. A spell's name gets the first line of its cell, with its badges under it. Clicking a name opens its definition under the row: key facts, Limitations, what it does, its Rank breaks and its description. Skills have a Limitations field, and Limitations and a written-out Range show in tooltips, on the item sheet and on the cast card. New example set: Terms Apply.
- **Definitions from chat.** A spell's or skill's name on a chat card posted from this release opens its definition in a small window, and the spell manager's cards open the same definition. A mob's items open only for the GM.
- **Combat riders.** A failed Throw, or an area attack marked to scatter, says how far it lands and offers a d8 roll for the direction. A weapon marked to reload needs one Action after a Major Fail and shows `[RELOAD]` until then. A weapon can name the ammunition it takes: each Attack spends from a matching stack, the picker preselects your last stack, and a new crawler with such a weapon starts with some. A Rank upgrade can give a free extra Attack, push, pull or slide the target (the card says how far, the GM moves the token, an immovable target says so), or read how far a mount moved this turn. Shooting without ammunition, unreloaded or a second extra Attack asks a Rule Break. New example set: Stage Hands.
- **PvP and NPC against NPC.** An attack on a player's crawler is Opposed: the attack total is the Difficulty of the victim's Evade. With the world setting **PvP rounds** on, the GM can turn on PvP rounds for a combat: each round the crawlers roll d20 + Dexterity for their order and take one Action plus Evade. A mob attacking another NPC is compared with its Evade and gets a GM-only d12 roll, no longer an automatic hit.
- **Movement.** The token ruler shows bands for Steps and Move Actions, and a token moved past its budget for the round warns its owner. The GM decides.
- **Exploring hazards.** A Harsh Conditions button on the Floor Collapse Clock rolls falling damage for the selected tokens. A travel pace (Fast, Normal or Slow) set on the clock changes the right Perception and Survival checks and asks a Rule Break for concealment at a Fast pace. Every 2 hours of travel past 10 in a day asks each crawler for an Endurance check. The world setting **Exploring hazards** turns these off. New example set: Scenic Route.
- **Checks.** After a Major or Critical Fail, rolling the same Skill again in that scene asks a Rule Break. The GM can set an escalating Difficulty (rising numbers such as three thresholds) and the card says which were reached.
- **Floors.** On a floor change the GM is offered the book's collapse time for the new floor, to take or ignore. A world that never set the Floor Collapse Clock now starts it at 150 hours (it was 100). A GM-only Adversary Power readout in the combat tracker gives the encounter's band from the party and the mobs (hidden while a boss is in the fight). Spending a Stat point before the Third Floor asks a Rule Break, and "once per X days" cooldowns are ready again on a new floor.
- **Crawler Creator.** Crawler numbers are drawn from the book's range. An animal crawler's size outside 1 to 5 asks a Rule Break, and a Human's typical size is a hint. A crawler who starts with no weapon and no attack spell holds a Bronze Weapon Box with the weapon left behind. A new Step 10 lists clothing, one interesting or useful item and weird stuff, can fill them from a kit, and names a unique weapon; a second useful item asks a Rule Break. A starter Crossbow, Handgun or Shotgun no longer adds Dexterity to its damage. New example set: Carry-On.
- **Story trackers.** The Story page holds a crawler's Top Ten place and shows its bounty from the Fourth Floor. Playing to the Cameras has the table's six results; the Sponsors column offers a held Sponsor Box (at most once per Floor, one tier lower after a Standard Fail, none after a Major Fail). A fourth Sponsor, Playing to the Sponsors before the Fourth Floor, and a second camera play inside a 2-hour session each ask a Rule Break. The contest tracker has a Skill Challenge kind: stages, each with its Skill and a GM-only Difficulty, and safety nets.
- **Minions.** A creature under a minion effect (or marked Minion in the Mob Creator) waits for a command: a Command button in the combat tracker spends its controller's Action, and the minion keeps its last order for the combat. When the controller dies its minions turn Bereft, and the GM's card can give control back. Effects aimed at a pet reach a minion, and a spell's split copies are commanded with an Action and an Unopposed Animal Handling check.
- **Loot, crafting and tithes.** An unfilled loot box pays its tier's usual gold. A box's spell row can teach a random Rank, and a scroll carries the Rank it casts at. Concoctions and infusions take minutes and read their own crafting table. A second enchantment on a tattoo asks a Rule Break, and a bonus can scale with the crawler's Level. A deity's tithe is worked out from the gold gained since the last offering; the GM can correct the count. New example set: Happy Hour.
- **Grinding by the book.** Every hour past 5 in a day needs an Unopposed Endurance check, and a failed hour earns nothing. A map's bonus hours count on any grind of 5 hours or more, unspent hours stay in the pool, and the Guide Insight hour is gone.
- **Race and Class builds.** The extra Build Points from detriments stop at 5, stat penalties included. Only Small or smaller sizes cost Build Points. The Passive Skill cap applies to Class builds only.
- **For macro and module authors.** [The module and macro author guide](docs/module-and-macro-api.md) lists everything on `game.cog`, `CONFIG.COG`, `window.cog`, the content registry and the system's hooks that a macro or module may rely on. A listed entry is removed or changes kind only after one release marks it deprecated; everything else is internal. `game.sprinterRpg` is the same object as `game.cog`.
- **Fixes.** Apply on a vehicle ram, thrown-pet or fall damage card deals its damage (it dealt 0). The round's free Evade shows as free in the tracker drawer and on attack cards, and no longer asks a Rule Break when both Actions are spent. A sheet open at login redraws when the imported races arrive, so its race list is complete. A combatant added after a combat starts gets its initiative. A mob's DR total counts modifier rows and transformations. A spell with no Range prints no Range line, where it printed "30 feet". A mob's flat check penalty lowers the Difficulty of an Opposed check against it, and the card names it. A player who pours a potion into another crawler, or buys or joins a Personal Space they cannot edit, has the GM check the rules and take the gold, once; a Rank Up Rule Break says what broke (too little gold, past the book, or both). A non-GM's item buff and minion command cards are checked against the item's Rank and the minion's controller.

## 8.3.0

Worlds from 8.0.0 or later upgrade when the GM loads them. This release converts two things: a crawler's unopened loot boxes move from its Items onto the crawler itself, and a hired mercenary loses an old flag nothing read.

- **Loot boxes live on the crawler.** An unopened box is no longer an Item in the inventory: a crawler holds its boxes as a list, shown on the same strip with the same Open all and GM controls. The GM edits a held box (name, image, tier, quantity, contents, "Keep sealed until filled") in a Loot Box window opened from the box's tile. Box templates stay loot items in the compendiums, and dropping one on a crawler gives it the box. A player can no longer drop a box template or an achievement onto their own crawler from a compendium or the Items sidebar. The PDF inventory lists held boxes. New example set: Prize Shelf.
- **Grinding tables.** The Grind window picks the Floor's or the neighbourhood's complication table, and a table row can change the hours credited or take time off the Floor Collapse Clock; the GM gets a card to apply the floor time. New example set: Backlot Grind.
- **Help and party checks.** Helping an ally adds +1d2 untrained or +1d6 trained before the roll, outside combat and not on Stat Checks; a Help pledge ends on a combat start or a scene change. A party check card counts the party's successes and applies the Amazing Success and Major Fail flips. New example set: Group Project.
- **Gear slot limits.** Equipping a second item in one slot, an eleventh accessory or a second belt asks a Rule Break, and the gear-slot grid shows an `[OVER]` badge. Capes go on the back or neck slot, and tattoos no longer count as accessories. New example set: Dress Code.
- **The body follows the form.** While a crawler is transformed, equipping gear the form cannot wear asks a Rule Break, the token takes the form's image and size, and the Size box shows the form's size beside the stored one. A transformation can post a cue at a rest or a new Floor. A changed body ends only by a cure, a Rule Break revert is recorded, and dragging a transformation from one sheet to another copies it. Cure all on a changed crawler no longer fails after the revert. New example set: Wardrobe Department.
- **Recharging.** A new "recharge" outcome restores a used-up item, junk that was once that item included, and an item's charges can come back on a rest or as world time passes. New example set: Refill Station.
- **Improvised weapons.** Any held item that is not a weapon can be swung on the Improvised Weapons Skill, from a list on the first sheet page.
- **Play page.** Temporary Health Bars show on the play page (a tap down still spends them first), a tap that lands on 0 Health asks once, an Evade is named "Evade" on the last-roll line, and a read-only Damage Resistance tile sits beside Evade.
- **Rule Break checks.** A Starter Spell needs Intelligence 4. Rolled stats ("Leaving it to Chance") are a Crawler Creator option. Rank 10 and 15 Upgrades start on the Third Floor. Magic-only Passive Skills cannot be ground. Add Pet links the pet only once the bond is confirmed. The skill-row badges are shorter and fit the name column.
- **Rules.** A standing Opposed Difficulty adds the foe's live Advantage shift at roll time. The Cat's Nine Lives opens on the first attack of the day. The crawler Cat and pet Cat races are told apart. Only Party Regeneration gives Regeneration to a member who has none. A buff that only grants a Skill, Spell or Rank is no longer reported as empty.
- **Fixes.** The Floor shows on the collapsed Floor Collapse Clock and no longer as a badge on chat cards. The crawler PDF's Mount and Pet boxes read the bonded vehicle and pet. A paid gig's AI Favor reroll asks its Rule Break. Typed text is escaped in every remaining chat card, dialog, sheet and tooltip. Item from Words keeps characters such as `<` and `&` as typed in plain fields.

## 8.2.0

Worlds from 8.0.0 or 8.1.0 upgrade on load; nothing stored changed shape in this release.

- **The play page.** A crawler sheet opens on a new first page, a one-screen view to run a turn from, built to fit a phone or a narrow window beside the map. It shows portrait, name, race, class, Level and Move; the 10 Health Bars (tap a bar to set Health to it) and Mana; the five Stat modifiers and Evade, each a roll button; AI Favor, which rerolls the last roll for 1 Favor; readied attacks with To Hit and Damage buttons; the filled hot bar slots; and a line with the last roll. It has no typed fields, so nothing changes by accident. **Full sheet** goes to the usual pages. The play page keeps its own window size per user, and the client setting **Open character sheets on the play page** picks where a sheet opens (by default players on the play page, the GM on the full sheet).
- **Attack Options window.** Maneuvers, Situations, Called Play and Cameras each collapse behind a bar, one entry per line, and open on their own when something in them arrives ticked; the Called Play bar counts pending plays.
- **Amazing Success on skills and spells.** A skill's or spell's own Amazing Success effects fire on its attacks, as a weapon's and a buff's do, and can be set from a Rank. A Natural 20 always hits. New example set: Second Unit.
- **Transformations.** A skill a form created is kept, not in force, across form switches, and at the revert the player is offered to keep any such skill they trained, at the Ranks earned. While changed, the original race's traits are paused (a race chosen while changed too), paused buffs add no tags, percent rows, caps and requirements read the changed body, and gear and buff bonuses count on a form's skills. Switching forms from the original body while another change is active asks a Rule Break.
- **Fixes.** The staged creators (Mob Creator, Crawler Creator) scroll on every stage with the footer always visible. The lifetime potion limits moved to the Inventory tab with a heading and an explanation, and the counts no longer wrap. Typed text such as a document name is escaped in skill, Stat, contest, patron, worship and vehicle damage cards.

## 8.1.0

Worlds saved on 8.0.0 upgrade when the GM loads them: the system converts the stored data once and keeps it intact. Pack document ids are unchanged.

- **World upgrades.** A world from 8.0.0 or later converts itself at load, run by the active GM, and players are told it happened. `cog.migrations.run({ dryRun: true })` at the console previews a run without writing. This release converts six things: a check row's `stat` check type becomes `skill`; the descriptive `rule.*` tags take their new ids; the unused `kind.upgrade` tag goes; canon races and mobs gain their creature-type tags; vehicle after-roll offers move onto the check record; a pet's old Floor text and banked grind hours go.
- **Mob Creator.** A new staged creator, laid out like the Crawler Creator: Identity, Stats, Defenses, Attacks, Abilities, Loot and Lore, Review, with the live stat block beside every stage. Fill to Budget spreads the stat points by a chosen focus; going over the budget, or past a rule, shows in the status line and asks a Rule Break on Create. Attacks, abilities (buffs), spells and loot are picked from the catalogs, with a New button for anything they lack; Loot and Lore sets the drops and the creature type. The old Mob Creator is gone. New example set: Creature Feature.
- **AI Favor rerolls and degree shifts.** Skill, Stat, attack and spell cards offer an AI Favor reroll once per check, before the result is applied, never on a Natural 1. Every check card records its roll, so a reroll or shift updates the Evade, damage, contest and vehicle results that read it. New example set: Second Takes.
- **Evade aftermath.** A Major Fail on Evade queues a Minor Injury for the end of combat and a Natural 1 a Major Injury; a Natural 20 or Amazing Success gives Advantage on your next attack against that mob, pre-ticked in Attack Options. Stunned, Queasy, Staggered and Shocked end after the bearer's next Action.
- **Spells advance by use.** Spells take a Used mark, join the advancement batch and the grind app, and follow the same Rank limits as Skills. The Spells tab has the Skills tab's grind strip and Advancement Check button.
- **Race and class perks that act.** Perks that were only descriptive tags now do something: defence triggers (retaliate, reflect on a chance, once per round or combat), event triggers on a kill, a heal or gold, Popularity on an Amazing Success or after a kill, size situations, Human perks (AI Favor DR, level-up AI Favor), auto windows, damage retype, extra Health Bar HP, heal bonuses, and side effects on a skill's use. Ten races and classes carry them, with party buffs. New example sets: Signature Moves, Side Effects May Include.
- **Pets and mounts.** Pets level with their crawler and spend their own Stat points; their Health Bar slots and Rank follow the book. Taming checks by attitude, a pet limit, roles that scale with the Floor, guiding a pet in combat, and dedicated mounts (one Action to mount or dismount, the ride check, Thrown). Riders below Riding 5 attack with Disadvantage. A Create Pet window sits beside the Mob Creator. Pet Health and Mana never read above their maximum. New example set: Animal Segment.
- **Potions.** A second potion inside two rounds is Potion Sickness (a Rule Break confirm that applies Poison); no potions while Dying; a healing potion can be drunk as an Interrupt; Stat and Skill potions keep a lifetime count; a crawler can spend an Action to pour a potion into a party member, Dying ones included. New example set: Bottle Service.
- **Loot after a fight.** The combat end card has a GM button for mob loot (junk and gold by Floor, per body) shared to the party; a boss kill offers its Boss Box by tier and the map. New example set: Boss Maps.
- **Popularity checklist.** At session, day and floor end the GM gets each crawler's Popularity changes, pre-ticked from the clock and the ledger, to apply, undo or edit; Fan Box milestones alert the GM and wait for a GM to open. New example set: Fan Mail.
- **Stock attack modifiers.** The attack dialog suggests and pre-ticks size difference, a ranged or spell attack inside melee reach, cover, an unseen attacker, dim light and darkness (from the scene's lights), with a Suggested badge; a world setting turns the window off. New example set: Location Shoot.
- **Buffs from skills and spells.** A skill or spell use card applies its buff to you, the party or allies in range, at the item's duration and Rank. Durations can last a scene, a Floor or until a rest; temporary Health Bars act as shields; a defence can cut incoming spell damage to the caster's Rank 1 base; Amazing Success triggers sit on buffs. Dropping a buff on a sheet applies it the same way. New example set: Craft Services.
- **Typed action blocks.** A debuff can block moving, casting, or everything except attacking and moving, each a Rule Break confirm.
- **Tags.** The descriptive `rule.*` tags moved to `perk.*` and `action.*`, three pairs of twins merged, every tag label is unique, and canon races and mobs carry their creature types. Ten stock `situation.*` tags and the `upgrade.barracks` tag are new. New example set: Prop Department (grind maps and weapon groups).
- **Fixes.** A Dying crawler's later death counts the downing hit's weapon for kill triggers and kill Popularity. A negative DR no longer adds damage. A flat bonus on a standby damage part counts once on a Critical Hit. A pet's role-granted attack starts at the Floor on every roll path. Mob tokens placed in one batch get their own numbers. A roll counts as in combat only for a combatant of the running fight. The Grind window's Floor starts at the world's Floor. A second Evade in a round while Dying asks a Rule Break from the tracker drawer too. Event-trigger lines name the debuff, not its identifier. Combat-long buff windows record the running combat. A spell whose upgrade adds a debuff on an Amazing Success no longer puts it on the target at every cast. In a world without imported books, the Crawler Creator's starter weapon Skill no longer raises a different Skill instead of adding its own.

## 8.0.0

**Breaking.** Stored fields changed with no migration, so worlds made with 7.0.0 should be recreated and books imported again: 25 text fields that were rich text are now plain text (actor notes and story fields, mob descriptions and specials, the notes on skills, spells, gear and loot, attack effects, achievement quotes and reward contents, sponsor gifts and notes); a weapon's damage is its Skill's Base Damage or its own, and the weapon type, weapon category, proficiency mode, selected Skill and damage part fields are gone; race and class prerequisites are requirement rows; a personal space has a rank in place of its tier and size; a skill's Can Gain Ranks box is replaced by advancement limits; an actor's free-text deity, boon and sin fields give way to worship records; and a mob imported from the books stores its printed DR minus its home Floor, so it reads the printed number on that Floor.

- **Item setup.** Creating an item anywhere (the Items directory, the Item, Skill and Spell managers, a sheet's "+" link) opens one setup window that asks that kind's essentials in plain words, for every item kind, attacks included. Every item sheet has a "What's missing" part with fix buttons, and creating an item that does nothing asks "Create anyway?". Item sheets label their fields in plain words with hints, the header names the item's type, tag pickers say what each weapon and kind tag means, and every row kind has a hint. A new buff's or debuff's duration text matches its timed duration.
- **Weapons.** A weapon lists the Skills it uses in order of preference, and the Attack Options window picks one per attack; an untrained Skill, or one the world lacks, rolls with Disadvantage. Damage is the Skill's Base Damage, growing at its Rank 5 and 10 upgrades, unless the weapon sets its own; bonus damage is the weapon's own rows. Starter, floor-loot and held weapons no longer count their base damage twice, starter weapon Skills carry their upgrade dice, and every shipped weapon has a Skill and a type. The gear sheet's weapon section opens by default and explains where the damage comes from; attack items use the same section, and a mob's attack sheet says what the mob really rolls. A guided **Advanced Weapon Builder** walks through Skills, type and hands, damage at Rank 0, 5 and 10, extras and a review. The Tag System journal's new "Make a Weapon That Works" page is built on real example documents. New example set: Weapon Workshop.
- **Untrained use.** A shipped weapon used untrained deals its Skill's Rank 0 damage instead of nothing, and the Session Manager offers the Skill, not the weapon's name, to train. Untrained use teaches one Skill per 2-hour session, on the Tutorial Floors only; a second one, or one on a later Floor, asks a Rule Break. The crawler's owning player picks the Skill when connected, the GM otherwise.
- **Skill Rank limits.** A Skill stops at Rank 10 until Race and Class are chosen and at 15 after; Ranks above 15 act as 15 until the Sixth Floor, with a badge on the skill row. Race and Class grants past the limit stop there with a Rule Break.
- **Skill advancement limits.** A Skill can limit how far each route (use, grinding, training, magic) raises it: one only magic can raise, one that use and grinding take only to a set Rank. The skill sheet's "How it gains Ranks" box sets them, with a Magic only preset; the skill row shows a lock badge, and going past a limit asks a Rule Break before anything is spent. New example set: Network Notes.
- **Race and Class requirements.** Races and classes carry requirement rows: a Stat score, a Skill Rank, Popularity, a Floor, another Race or Class, an achievement, a tag, or something the GM judges. The Crawler Creator shows them and puts the Stat step after Race and Class so points can be moved to meet them; an unmet requirement asks a Rule Break. New example set: Fine Print.
- **Crawler Creator.** A write-in experience picks a real Skill or Spell from the catalog. Roll Everything Remaining answers every open pick and die to the end, and Randomize All rolls the whole draft again. A random Level 1 crawler never picks the same Skill twice across its life stages. A crawler has a description, set in the creator, and the example crawlers have one.
- **Personal spaces.** A space has a rank and holds two tables of any type per rank; more asks a Rule Break. It ranks up with an upgrade coupon or its price, which the GM sets per rank, and party members can join their spaces with a connector for a shared rank. Studios (rank 2 and up, taking no table space) change crafting at one table type: a check bonus, less time or fewer materials, batches, salvage, or recipes they unlock. Buy a Table and loot bring tables, the Stash Manager holds carried tables without using a slot, a spent coupon leaves nothing behind, and every purchase asks who pays. The Upgrades tab lists joined spaces' tables and studios with Craft here. New example set: Open Plan.
- **Deities.** A deity records its god's title, temple, daily offering, rival, signature Skills and Stat, symbol and benefits by church Tier. A crawler worships a god they know from the sheet's deity block; daily offerings keep a streak, a missed day suspends the god's benefits until the next offering, and a longer lapse brings a debuff. Each Tier adds the god's benefits as buffs. The GM grants boons and smites from the deity row and records offenses, and harming a fellow worshipper counts as one; each player sees markers on the tokens of fellow and rival worshippers, and the GM is told when a rival's worshipper arrives on a scene. A class can require worship. Buffs can now raise a Skill or Spell's Rank, switch on for a window once a day, or give immunity, and the buff sheet edits Skill and rank-raise rows. A rulebook import supplies the book's sample gods and its divine debuffs. New example set: Prime Time Pantheon.
- **Bard Patronage.** A new Patron item. A crawler whose class allows it takes on a patron, offered at class selection or through the GM's Audition, a run of skill checks the card tracks; Make Patron lists every criterion as met, unmet or for the GM to judge, and a god can be a patron. Gigs earn money through the patron, patrons give advice, quests and favours, and mentorship gives Advantage when grinding. Patronage ends when either side quits or dies, with notices to the GM. A rulebook import supplies the book's example patrons. New example set: Green Room.
- **Race and class studios on the crawler.** The crawler sheet's race and class "+" links open the studio for that crawler, and Create puts the new race or class on it, with a whisper to the GM.
- **Dying.** At 0% Health a crawler or mercenary is Dying: a countdown from their Con Mod loses one at each round's Clean Up and with each hit, keeps running with the world clock outside combat, and ends in death at zero. Healing a Health Bar slot stabilises them. Acting while Dying or dead (an Action, an Evade, a Stat or Skill Check, a spell or an item) asks a Rule Break that names who and why. Mobs, pets and NPCs die at 0%. Dying and Dead badges show on the sheet, the combat tracker and the HUD; undoing a hit gives the countdown back; kill credit and Popularity come at death, not at 0%. New example set: Final Credits.
- **Difficulty.** Plain Skill and Stat check cards have one-click Opposed, Unopposed and Stat Check Difficulty buttons for the current Floor, or a typed number, so degrees, camera awards and assists resolve at once. The Session Manager ledger has the same buttons, and the GM can put up a standing Difficulty that stamps every plain check rolled while it is up. A mob's flat check bonuses and penalties move the Difficulty as its Advantage does.
- **Standard debuffs.** The standard debuffs do what the rules say: stackable ones stack, a second injury of a grade becomes its long-term form and a third asks the GM, and a debuff can bring another with it. Debuffs can stop Actions (asking a Rule Break), block healing or last until healed, give attackers Advantage, halve Move, or end on the bearer's next check or Action; one that lasts to the end of the next round now lasts all of it. New example set: Technical Difficulties.
- **Evade, Critical Hits, mob DR.** The first Evade of a round spends the Action and later Evades that round are free. A Critical Hit rolls the base damage dice twice; Stat Mod and flat bonuses count once and the Rank die is not doubled. A mob's DR never drops below 0.
- **Linked characters.** Crawler, pet and mercenary tokens are always linked to their one actor: a mercenary token can no longer be unlinked, a token pointed at one of them is linked, and the GM's client relinks any that arrive unlinked on a new scene or at load. A sheet edit on such a token writes its actor once.
- **Plain text.** Notes and the other plain fields save and show exactly what was typed, with no stray `<p>` tags. Imported book text keeps characters such as `<`, and imported table and vehicle names keep theirs.
- **Inventory groups.** The crawler inventory is grouped by item category (weapons, armor, accessories, tattoos, patches, potions and consumables, scrolls, wands, lottery tickets, blueprints, other), and the item picker filters by the same categories. The actor tag editor moved to the bottom of page 1.
- **Transformations.** A sheet save or a race choice while changed no longer stores the form's size and Move on the actor. The original race shows as not in force, choosing a race while changed says which race the crawler returns to, Add form shows its row, and editing a changed affliction goes through the cure window. A used item's debuff lands with its card and secret hold, and token vision follows the change.
- Typed chat commands (`/themes`, `/guild`, `/rlog`, `/log`) work again on Foundry 14.
- A name with an apostrophe or ampersand shows as typed on dialog buttons and window titles.
- A new world with no scenes gets an Earth: Surface scene.
- Button labels no longer spill out of their buttons or break mid-word.
- The grind window pre-ticks the party's lowest-Level member for the extra 1d2 Levels; a tie ticks nobody.
- The `[OFFLINE]` badge shows only on gear that gives something the zero zone turns off, readable in both themes, and Skills granted by gear no longer read as offline outside a zero zone.
- Editing a space upgrade, sponsor, attack, patron or deity in a compendium updates the pickers.
- Known issues: typed text such as a document name containing markup is not yet escaped in every chat card. A free Evade from the combat tracker's drawer does not ask the Dying Rule Break, a mob's attack sheet Rank table still reads as a crawler's, and the bundled pets' attack Skills have no Rank upgrades.
- Under the hood: new structure checks keep document type words, setting keys and flag names spelled one way, the system's own flags go through shared accessors, typed text in cards goes through one escaper, and a change that needs a live check can ship behind a GM setting marked "Being tested", which a packaged build hides.

## 7.0.0

**Breaking.** Some fields were removed or moved with no migration, so worlds made with 6.0.0 should be recreated: gear's five Stat bonus boxes are now Stat bonus rows, the crafting workstation level and craft fields are gone, and vehicles no longer carry a copy of the crawler's fields.

- **Transformations.** A debuff can be an affliction that changes its victim: into a form (a race made in the Race Creator with the new Transformation form box), with added buffs and debuffs, or both. The change happens when someone clicks Change, from the landing card or the conditions row (the sheet's banner names the form and has no controls); the original race stays underneath and comes back on Revert. Alternate forms (a werewolf's wolf and human shapes), a cure window per affliction (never, always, or until the end of the floor; past it a cure asks a Rule Break), a GM-only card when a carrier drops to 0 Health, and stat rows with a Minimum ("−10 Charisma, not below 2"). A debuff marked Secret is held by the GM until revealed; the Session Manager's new Secrets tab lists every secret and pending affliction. A paused debuff does nothing until switched on. New example set: Contract Clauses.
- **Turn a theme off.** A new **Themes Turned Off** world setting removes a theme's content (say Shiny, after a rulebook import) from the pickers, creators, managers and compendium sidebar. After the reload the GM is offered a **Theme Cleanup**: documents of a turned-off theme already on actors can be deleted or replaced with Celestial AI Poop, which the GM can restore later. `/themes` opens it again.
- **Homebrew badge.** A document made in a world gets a Homebrew badge naming the user who made it.
- **AI-written content.** Every document carries who wrote its words: a person, AI, or AI edited by a person, shown as a mark beside the source badge and changed from the sheet's Authorship control. The shipped examples are marked AI-written. A new **AI-Written Content** world setting can hide AI-written content, or AI-written and human-edited content, everywhere the theme switch does; the cleanup offers to replace hidden documents already on actors. With nothing human-written installed, the race and class lists are empty until you import your books or make your own.
- **Zero zone.** The GM switches on a world-wide zero zone (Token controls, a keybinding, or a Scene Region with the Zero Zone behavior) where crawlers' enhancements stop working: every player's HUD goes dark, equipped gear goes offline unless tagged Works in Zero Zone, buffs and debuffs pause, and casting a spell, using an item or attacking asks a Rule Break. Players are never told why. The sheet shows which gear is offline. New example set: Offline Mode.
- **Hide the HUD.** A player hides their crawler HUD and hotlist from the Token controls or with Shift+H; the sheet keeps them.
- **Loot boxes and achievements are the GM's.** Boxes sit in a Loot Boxes strip, not the inventory; players can only Open all, and an opened box leaves nothing behind. Boxes never go into or out of a stash. The safe room is a scene tagged as one, nothing else.
- **Token defaults.** Every new actor's token shows its name to everyone; crawlers, NPCs, pets and mercenaries also get vision with a 120 ft range. Compendium companions and example actors carry the same defaults.
- **The action ledger asks.** An action past a combatant's budget, or a regular mob's second attack, asks a Rule Break first.
- **Combat.** Next Step and Previous Step hotkeys (Shift+Period, Shift+Comma), and End Round & Reset at Step 5. The Called Play discard button works.
- **Rich-text descriptions.** Race, class, gear, skill and every other item sheet edits its description in the rich-text editor instead of showing raw HTML.
- **Item sheets edit every example field.** The Stat Bonuses table has a Label column; the Light section shows on any item with a light; outcome rows gain a plain damage type, a filter for Cure Debuff and spell damage controls; skill rank tiers gain Rank Dice Source and Temp Bars Granted; Delivery and gear slot choices come from their registries, so a module's `delivery.*` tags and gear slots appear.
- The Cure Debuff outcome picks the debuff it cures; the old text box did nothing.
- The Item Manager merges entries by their source, not their name.
- The floor-start bracelet rolls its race on its own table, and the arrival card links to write-in picks.
- The day clock uses the 30-hour dungeon day everywhere.
- Long and full-day rests never lower Health or Mana that is above its maximum.
- Bundled mercenaries' weapons use real weapon categories.
- Dark theme: unchecked checkboxes are visible in every system window and chat card.
- The crawler HUD appears for a token controlled before the world finished loading.
- **Crafting.** A crafting window, opened from the crawler's inventory, a blueprint or a personal space, runs the crafting check: materials come from the crawler first, then their space; the margin decides salvage; the result card lists what was made. Enchantments, broken gear and Repair, crafting specialties, crafting tables bought by tier, and Misc. Junk to spend or pull a common item from. Crafting rules live on skills as rows (caps by Rank, half-time upgrades). New example set: Crafting.
- **Vehicles.** Vehicle checks with after-roll offers (reroll, degree shift), driving, boarding, ramming and explosions, vehicle combat (attack Difficulty, occupant Evade, vehicle DR), mounted weapons fired by an occupant, and a vehicle sheet with an Aboard strip. A vehicle's Health follows its size. New example set: Vehicles.
- **Loot boxes.** An achievement that rewards a box gives the crawler a real box item. The GM fills boxes with items, spells, skill potions, money and consumables, or with choices the crawler picks from. In a safe room the crawler opens their boxes in the Loot Box Showcase, lowest tier first. Boxes can be pre-rolled, and an item shows where it came from. New example set: Loot Boxes.
- **Percent Stat bonuses.** Gear, buffs and debuffs give flat and percent Stat bonuses as rows ("+5 STR, +10% STR"). A world setting picks whether gear percents are worked out once when received (the default) or kept live; buff and debuff percents are always live.
- **Floor 3, 4 and 5 starts in the Crawler Creator.** The creator builds a later-floor crawler in its own steps, with a preview that follows the choices; the separate Floor Simulator window is gone. The footer says what Create will do. New example set: Floor Starts.
- **Content themes and source badges.** Every installed theme's content is in the pickers and compendiums at once; a new **Primary Content Theme** setting (it replaces Active Content Theme) picks which one supplies the crawler creator's species, backgrounds and story tables. The examples theme is merged into Shiny, whose compendiums are now Shiny Items, Shiny Actors and Shiny Tables. ## 6.0.0

**Breaking.** The compendiums now ship only content written for this system. Every entry from the source books is left out and comes from a Sourcery import of your own PDFs. Worlds made with 5.11.0 should be recreated.

- A base set that works without an import: ten classes (Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Monk, Bard, Mage, Cleric, Paladin, Druid, Arcanist), two races (Human, and Non-Human, which is deliberately unbalanced and asks for a Rule Break), 68 skills, 8 spells and 10 debuffs. Every class pick offers at least one more option than it needs.
- The Classes system compendium is gone; the base classes are in the example compendiums, and an import writes the books' classes to its own world compendium.
- An import writes a book entry only when your PDF has it, and its review lists how many were left out. Importing again adds to the last import: a book picked again replaces what was read from it, and books left out keep what they gave.
- Floor-table rewards that name a book entry keep their text, find a base document of the same name before an import, and are marked "needs an import" when nothing matches. They can still be picked.
- Debuffs are found by identifier or condition tag, never by name.
- The scrolls, wand, ring and tooth caps that cast or granted book spells and skills now use base ones.
- The Forge: install by manifest, Game Manager, socketlib, the relay and the Sourcery import are tested on a real Forge account (see `docs/hosting-on-the-forge.md`).

## 5.11.0

First public release of Sprinter RPG, a Foundry VTT system (v14 and later, with socketlib).

- Crawler, mob, pet and vehicle sheets, with health bars, skills, spells, buffs and debuffs, gear and loot.
- Creators for crawlers, races, classes, mobs and items, each built on a pure `buildPayload(spec)` compiler.
- A tag system that carries tiers, damage types and conditions, with registries that modules can extend.
- Original example content, in compendium packs.
- The Sourcery importer, which turns the owner's own PDFs into world compendiums with the book text, in the browser.
- Fillable-sheet PDF export from a sheet the GM supplies.
