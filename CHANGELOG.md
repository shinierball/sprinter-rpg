# Changelog

## 8.0.0

**Breaking.** Stored fields changed with no migration, so worlds made with 7.0.0 should be recreated and books imported again: 25 text fields that were rich text are now plain text (actor notes and story fields, mob descriptions and specials, the notes on skills, spells, gear and loot, attack effects, achievement quotes and reward contents, sponsor gifts and notes); a weapon's damage is its Skill's Base Damage or its own, and the weapon type, weapon category, proficiency mode, selected Skill and damage part fields are gone; race and class prerequisites are requirement rows; a personal space has a rank in place of its tier and size; a skill's Can Gain Ranks box is replaced by advancement limits; an actor's free-text deity, boon and sin fields give way to worship records; and a mob imported from the books stores its printed DR minus its home Floor, so it reads the printed number on that Floor.

- **Item setup.** Creating an item anywhere (the Items directory, the Item, Skill and Spell managers, a sheet's "+" link) opens one setup window that asks that kind's essentials in plain words, for every item kind, attacks included. Every item sheet has a "What's missing" part with fix buttons, and creating an item that does nothing asks "Create anyway?". Item sheets label their fields in plain words with hints, the header names the item's type, tag pickers say what each weapon and kind tag means, and every row kind has a hint. A new buff's or debuff's duration text matches its timed duration.
- **Weapons.** A weapon lists the Skills it uses in order of preference, and the Attack Options window picks one per attack; an untrained Skill, or one the world lacks, rolls with Disadvantage. Damage is the Skill's Base Damage, growing at its Rank 5 and 10 upgrades, unless the weapon sets its own; bonus damage is the weapon's own rows. Starter, floor-loot and held weapons no longer count their base damage twice, starter weapon Skills carry their upgrade dice, and every shipped weapon has a Skill and a type. The gear sheet's weapon section opens by default and explains where the damage comes from; attack items use the same section, and a mob's attack sheet says what the mob really rolls. A guided **Advanced Weapon Builder** walks through Skills, type and hands, damage at Rank 0, 5 and 10, extras and a review. The Tag System journal's new "Make a Weapon That Works" page is built on real example documents. New example set: Weapon Workshop.
- **Untrained use.** A shipped weapon used untrained deals its Skill's Rank 0 damage instead of nothing, and the Session Manager offers the Skill, not the weapon's name, to train. Untrained use teaches one Skill per 2-hour session, on the Tutorial Floors only; a second one, or one on a later Floor, asks a Rule Break. The crawler's owning player picks the Skill when connected, the GM otherwise.
- **Skill Rank limits.** A Skill stops at Rank 10 until Race and Class are chosen and at 15 after; Ranks above 15 act as 15 until the Sixth Floor, with a badge on the skill row. Race and Class grants past the limit stop there with a Rule Break.
- **Skill advancement limits.** A Skill can limit how far each route (use, grinding, training, magic) raises it: one only magic can raise, one that use and grinding take only to a set Rank. The skill sheet's "How it gains Ranks" box sets them, with a Magic only preset; the skill row shows a lock badge, and going past a limit asks a Rule Break before anything is spent. New example set: Network Notes.
- **Race and Class requirements.** Races and classes carry requirement rows: a Stat score, a Skill Rank, Popularity, a Floor, another Race or Class, an achievement, a tag, or something the GM judges. The Crawler Creator shows them and puts the Stat step after Race and Class so points can be moved to meet them; an unmet requirement asks a Rule Break. New example set: Fine Print.
- **Crawler Creator.** A write-in experience picks a real Skill or Spell from the catalog. Roll Everything Remaining answers every open pick and die to the end, and Randomize All rolls the whole draft again. A random Level 1 crawler never picks the same Skill twice across its life stages. A crawler has a description, set in the creator, and the example crawlers have one.
- **Personal spaces.** A space has a rank and holds two tables of any type per rank; more asks a Rule Break. It ranks up with an upgrade coupon or its price, which the GM sets per rank, and party members can join their spaces with a connector for a shared rank. Studios (rank 2 and up, taking no table space) change crafting at one table type: a check bonus, less time or fewer materials, batches, salvage, or recipes they unlock. Buy a Table and loot bring tables, the Stash Manager holds carried tables without using a slot, a spent coupon leaves nothing behind, and every purchase asks who pays. The Upgrades tab lists joined spaces' tables and studios with Craft here. New example set: Open Plan.
- **Deities.** A deity records its god's title, temple, daily offering, rival, signature Skills and Stat, symbol and benefits by church Tier. A crawler worships a god they know from the sheet's deity block; daily offerings keep a streak, a missed day suspends the god's benefits until the next offering, and a longer lapse brings a debuff. Each Tier adds the god's benefits as buffs. The GM grants boons and smites from the deity row and records offenses, and harming a fellow worshipper counts as one; each player sees markers on the tokens of fellow and rival worshippers, and the GM is told when a rival's worshipper arrives on a scene. A class can require worship. Buffs can now raise a Skill or Spell's Rank, switch on for a window once a day, or give immunity, and the buff sheet edits Skill and rank-raise rows. A rulebook import supplies the book's sample gods and its divine debuffs. New example set: Prime Time Pantheon.
- **Bard Patronage.** A new Patron item. A crawler whose class allows it takes on a patron, offered at class selection or through the GM's Audition, a run of skill checks the card tracks; Make Patron lists every criterion as met, unmet or for the GM to judge, and a god can be a patron. Gigs earn money through the patron, patrons give advice, quests and favours, and mentorship gives Advantage when grinding. Patronage ends when either side quits or dies, with notices to the GM. A rulebook import supplies the book's example patrons. New example set: Green Room.
- **Race and class studios on the crawler.** The crawler sheet's race and class "+" links open the studio for that crawler, and Create puts the new race or class on it, with a whisper to the GM.
- **Dying.** At 0% Health a crawler or mercenary is Dying: a countdown from their Con Mod loses one at each round's Clean Up and with each hit, keeps running with the world clock outside combat, and ends in death at zero. Healing a Health Bar slot stabilises them. Acting while Dying or dead (an Action, an Evade, a Stat or Skill Check, a spell or an item) asks a Rule Break that names who and why. Mobs, pets and NPCs die at 0%. Dying and Dead badges show on the sheet, the combat tracker and the HUD; undoing a hit gives the countdown back; kill credit and Popularity come at death, not at 0%. New example set: Final Credits.
- **Difficulty.** Plain Skill and Stat check cards have one-click Opposed, Unopposed and Stat Check Difficulty buttons for the current Floor, or a typed number, so degrees, camera awards and assists resolve at once. The Session Manager ledger has the same buttons, and the GM can put up a standing Difficulty that stamps every plain check rolled while it is up. A mob's flat check bonuses and penalties move the Difficulty as its Advantage does.
- **Standard debuffs.** The standard debuffs do what the rules say: stackable ones stack, a second injury of a grade becomes its long-term form and a third asks the GM, and a debuff can bring another with it. Debuffs can stop Actions (asking a Rule Break), block healing or last until healed, give attackers Advantage, halve Move, or end on the bearer's next check or Action; one that lasts to the end of the next round now lasts all of it. New example set: Technical Difficulties.
- **Evade, Critical Hits, mob DR.** The first Evade of a round spends the Action and later Evades that round are free. A Critical Hit rolls the base damage dice twice; Stat Mod and flat bonuses count once and the Rank die is not doubled. A mob's DR never drops below 0.
- **Linked characters.** Crawler, pet and mercenary tokens are always linked to their one actor: a mercenary token can no longer be unlinked, a token pointed at one of them is linked, and the GM's client relinks any that arrive unlinked on a new scene or at load. A sheet edit on such a token writes its actor once.
- **Plain text.** Notes and the other plain fields save and show exactly what was typed, with no stray `<p>` tags. Imported book text keeps characters such as `<`, and imported table and vehicle names keep theirs.
- **Inventory groups.** The crawler inventory is grouped by item category (weapons, armor, accessories, tattoos, patches, potions and consumables, scrolls, wands, lottery tickets, blueprints, other), and the item picker filters by the same categories. The actor tag editor moved to the bottom of page 1.
- **Transformations.** A sheet save or a race choice while changed no longer stores the form's size and Move on the actor. The original race shows as not in force, choosing a race while changed says which race the crawler returns to, Add form shows its row, and editing a changed affliction goes through the cure window. A used item's debuff lands with its card and secret hold, and token vision follows the change.
- Typed chat commands (`/themes`, `/guild`, `/rlog`, `/log`) work again on Foundry 14.
- A name with an apostrophe or ampersand shows as typed on dialog buttons and window titles.
- A new world with no scenes gets an Earth: Surface scene.
- Button labels no longer spill out of their buttons or break mid-word.
- The grind window pre-ticks the party's lowest-Level member for the extra 1d2 Levels; a tie ticks nobody.
- The `[OFFLINE]` badge shows only on gear that gives something the zero zone turns off, readable in both themes, and Skills granted by gear no longer read as offline outside a zero zone.
- Editing a space upgrade, sponsor, attack, patron or deity in a compendium updates the pickers.
- Known issues: typed text such as a document name containing markup is not yet escaped in every chat card. A free Evade from the combat tracker's drawer does not ask the Dying Rule Break, a mob's attack sheet Rank table still reads as a crawler's, and the bundled pets' attack Skills have no Rank upgrades.
- Under the hood: new structure checks keep document type words, setting keys and flag names spelled one way, the system's own flags go through shared accessors, typed text in cards goes through one escaper, and a change that needs a live check can ship behind a GM setting marked "Being tested", which a packaged build hides.

## 7.0.0

**Breaking.** Some fields were removed or moved with no migration, so worlds made with 6.0.0 should be recreated: gear's five Stat bonus boxes are now Stat bonus rows, the crafting workstation level and craft fields are gone, and vehicles no longer carry a copy of the crawler's fields.

- **Transformations.** A debuff can be an affliction that changes its victim: into a form (a race made in the Race Creator with the new Transformation form box), with added buffs and debuffs, or both. The change happens when someone clicks Change, from the landing card, the conditions row or the sheet's banner; the original race stays underneath and comes back on Revert. Alternate forms (a werewolf's wolf and human shapes), a cure window per affliction (never, always, or until the end of the floor; past it a cure asks a Rule Break), a GM-only card when a carrier drops to 0 Health, and stat rows with a Minimum ("−10 Charisma, not below 2"). A debuff marked Secret is held by the GM until revealed; the Session Manager's new Secrets tab lists every secret and pending affliction. A paused debuff does nothing until switched on. New example set: Contract Clauses.
- **Turn a theme off.** A new **Themes Turned Off** world setting removes a theme's content (say Shiny, after a rulebook import) from the pickers, creators, managers and compendium sidebar. After the reload the GM is offered a **Theme Cleanup**: documents of a turned-off theme already on actors can be deleted or replaced with Celestial AI Poop, which the GM can restore later. `/themes` opens it again.
- **Homebrew badge.** A document made in a world gets a Homebrew badge naming the user who made it.
- **AI-written content.** Every document carries who wrote its words: a person, AI, or AI edited by a person, shown as a mark beside the source badge and changed from the sheet's Authorship control. The shipped examples are marked AI-written. A new **AI-Written Content** world setting can hide AI-written content, or AI-written and human-edited content, everywhere the theme switch does; the cleanup offers to replace hidden documents already on actors. With nothing human-written installed, the race and class lists are empty until you import your books or make your own.
- **Zero zone.** The GM switches on a world-wide zero zone (Token controls, a keybinding, or a Scene Region with the Zero Zone behavior) where crawlers' enhancements stop working: every player's HUD goes dark, equipped gear goes offline unless tagged Works in Zero Zone, buffs and debuffs pause, and casting a spell, using an item or attacking asks a Rule Break. Players are never told why. The sheet shows which gear is offline. New example set: Offline Mode.
- **Hide the HUD.** A player hides their crawler HUD and hotlist from the Token controls or with Shift+H; the sheet keeps them.
- **Loot boxes and achievements are the GM's.** Boxes sit in a Loot Boxes strip, not the inventory; players can only Open all, and an opened box leaves nothing behind. Boxes never go into or out of a stash. The safe room is a scene tagged as one, nothing else.
- **Token defaults.** Every new actor's token shows its name to everyone; crawlers, NPCs, pets and mercenaries also get vision with a 120 ft range. Compendium companions and example actors carry the same defaults.
- **The action ledger asks.** An action past a combatant's budget, or a regular mob's second attack, asks a Rule Break first.
- **Combat.** Next Step and Previous Step hotkeys (Shift+Period, Shift+Comma), and End Round & Reset at Step 5. The Called Play discard button works.
- **Rich-text descriptions.** Race, class, gear, skill and every other item sheet edits its description in the rich-text editor instead of showing raw HTML.
- **Item sheets edit every example field.** The Stat Bonuses table has a Label column; the Light section shows on any item with a light; outcome rows gain a plain damage type, a filter for Cure Debuff and spell damage controls; skill rank tiers gain Rank Dice Source and Temp Bars Granted; Delivery and gear slot choices come from their registries, so a module's `delivery.*` tags and gear slots appear.
- The Cure Debuff outcome picks the debuff it cures; the old text box did nothing.
- The Item Manager merges entries by their source, not their name.
- The floor-start bracelet rolls its race on its own table, and the arrival card links to write-in picks.
- The day clock uses the 30-hour dungeon day everywhere.
- Long and full-day rests never lower Health or Mana that is above its maximum.
- Bundled mercenaries' weapons use real weapon categories.
- Dark theme: unchecked checkboxes are visible in every system window and chat card.
- The crawler HUD appears for a token controlled before the world finished loading.
- **Crafting.** A crafting window, opened from the crawler's inventory, a blueprint or a personal space, runs the crafting check: materials come from the crawler first, then their space; the margin decides salvage; the result card lists what was made. Enchantments, broken gear and Repair, crafting specialties, crafting tables bought by tier, and Misc. Junk to spend or pull a common item from. Crafting rules live on skills as rows (caps by Rank, half-time upgrades). New example set: Crafting.
- **Vehicles.** Vehicle checks with after-roll offers (reroll, degree shift), driving, boarding, ramming and explosions, vehicle combat (attack Difficulty, occupant Evade, vehicle DR), mounted weapons fired by an occupant, and a vehicle sheet with an Aboard strip. A vehicle's Health follows its size. New example set: Vehicles.
- **Loot boxes.** An achievement that rewards a box gives the crawler a real box item. The GM fills boxes with items, spells, skill potions, money and consumables, or with choices the crawler picks from. In a safe room the crawler opens their boxes in the Loot Box Showcase, lowest tier first. Boxes can be pre-rolled, and an item shows where it came from. New example set: Loot Boxes.
- **Percent Stat bonuses.** Gear, buffs and debuffs give flat and percent Stat bonuses as rows ("+5 STR, +10% STR"). A world setting picks whether gear percents are worked out once when received (the default) or kept live; buff and debuff percents are always live.
- **Floor 3, 4 and 5 starts in the Crawler Creator.** The creator builds a later-floor crawler in its own steps, with a preview that follows the choices; the separate Floor Simulator window is gone. The footer says what Create will do. New example set: Floor Starts.
- **Content themes and source badges.** Every installed theme's content is in the pickers and compendiums at once; a new **Primary Content Theme** setting (it replaces Active Content Theme) picks which one supplies the crawler creator's species, backgrounds and story tables. The examples theme is merged into Shiny, whose compendiums are now Shiny Items, Shiny Actors and Shiny Tables. Each document shows a set-symbol badge for where it came from (Shiny and its set, a book from your import, or the bundled documents) on sheets, lists, pickers and compendium entries. A borrowed-text import is badged with the book whose PDF supplied the text. Module authors: see `docs/content-themes.md`.
- **Sex on the character sheet.** Crawler and mob sheets have a Sex select (Male, Female, Non-binary, Other) that sets the actor's own sex tag; a sex that comes from a race or other item is shown as a hint. New tags `creature.nonbinary` and `creature.sex_other`.
- The creator's starter mana potion, Green Room Mana Spritzer, is a real Shiny document with its badge.
- Loot and gear outcomes offer Restore Resource in their type selects; editing a mana-restore item no longer turns it into a heal.
- Gear auras pick their buffs, debuffs and pulse conditions from the same pickers as the rest of the sheet, not from comma lists.
- System hotbar macros in an older world are rewritten to match the system's code.
- A compendium sync keeps a crawler's race and class picks.
- The importer reads the books' crafting, vehicle and loot box rows, and the Crawler Creator waits for an import's catalogs before it builds.
- Arrival cards tell Dice So Nice not to roll dice already shown in the creator.
- Under the hood: the code was reorganised (engines moved out of the windows, actor behaviour as plain functions, `src/utils/` grouped by feature, long modules split) with no change to play. The stylesheet is now thirteen files under `styles/`, listed in `system.json`; a module that overrode `styles/cog.css` by path should target the new files.

## 6.0.0

**Breaking.** The compendiums now ship only content written for this system. Every entry from the source books is left out and comes from a Sourcery import of your own PDFs. Worlds made with 5.11.0 should be recreated.

- A base set that works without an import: ten classes (Fighter, Barbarian, Rogue, Monk, Bard, Mage, Cleric, Paladin, Druid, Arcanist), two races (Human, and Non-Human, which is deliberately unbalanced and asks for a Rule Break), 68 skills, 8 spells and 10 debuffs. Every class pick offers at least one more option than it needs.
- The Classes system compendium is gone; the base classes are in the example compendiums, and an import writes the books' classes to its own world compendium.
- An import writes a book entry only when your PDF has it, and its review lists how many were left out. Importing again adds to the last import: a book picked again replaces what was read from it, and books left out keep what they gave.
- Floor-table rewards that name a book entry keep their text, find a base document of the same name before an import, and are marked "needs an import" when nothing matches. They can still be picked.
- Debuffs are found by identifier or condition tag, never by name.
- The scrolls, wand, ring and tooth caps that cast or granted book spells and skills now use base ones.
- The Forge: install by manifest, Game Manager, socketlib, the relay and the Sourcery import are tested on a real Forge account (see `docs/hosting-on-the-forge.md`).

## 5.11.0

First public release of Sprinter RPG, a Foundry VTT system (v14 and later, with socketlib).

- Crawler, mob, pet and vehicle sheets, with health bars, skills, spells, buffs and debuffs, gear and loot.
- Creators for crawlers, races, classes, mobs and items, each built on a pure `buildPayload(spec)` compiler.
- A tag system that carries tiers, damage types and conditions, with registries that modules can extend.
- Original example content, in compendium packs.
- The Sourcery importer, which turns the owner's own PDFs into world compendiums with the book text, in the browser.
- Fillable-sheet PDF export from a sheet the GM supplies.
